Help us sharpen the questions. This is the whole v0.1 item set with every model's answer. We're looking for: questions that read as ambiguous or wrong, distractors that are too easy, too tricky, or mislabeled, and answer keys you think are incorrect. The “frontier missed” filter surfaces items even the strongest models got wrong — those are the most likely to have a problem. Found one? Tap Copy link on the item and text it over.
How to read a card Each multiple-choice item shows all choices with the correct one in green, each choice's distractor type, and a bar for how many model-answers picked it (so you can see which trap models fell for). Open-ended items show the prompt, the scoring rubric, and each model's actual written answer with its judge scores. The number on the right of each card is the item's difficulty: % of model-answers correct for MC, mean judge score /100 for open.
