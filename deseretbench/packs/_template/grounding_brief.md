# Grounding brief — TODO: <your tradition>

This file is the factual anchor embedded in every authoring prompt. It is what
keeps generated questions accurate and keyed to official sources instead of to
the model's half-remembered priors. Replace everything below with your
tradition's brief.

Write it dense and specific. Good briefs include:

- **Canon / authoritative texts** — what counts as scripture or binding, and the
  order of authority when sources conflict.
- **Official teaching bodies** — who speaks authoritatively (a magisterium, a
  synod, a first presidency, a body of jurists), and how current teaching is
  promulgated (handbooks, catechisms, encyclicals, responsa).
- **The mainstream position** on the doctrines your dimensions cover, stated
  plainly, with the distinction between settled doctrine and open questions.
- **Common confusions** — where this tradition is routinely conflated with a
  neighbouring one, and the precise difference. These become your distractors.
- **Vocabulary** — insider terms, their exact meanings, and where outsiders get
  them wrong.
- **Currency notes** — recent changes in practice or teaching, with dates, so
  authored items reflect the tradition as it is now rather than decades ago.

Keep it to what an expert author would need at their elbow. The LDS brief at
`data/grounding_brief.md` is a worked example of the right density and tone.
