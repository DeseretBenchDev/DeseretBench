# DeseretBench — Factual Grounding Brief (v0.1)

Verified facts compiled to ground question authoring and anchor the answer keys.
Currency-sensitive items are dated. Citations are to official Church sources where
possible (newsroom / churchofjesuschrist.org / Church News), supplemented by major
outlets. **As of June 2026.**

> Authors: key the *mainstream, official, correlated* position. Folk doctrine,
> heterodox ("Sunstone"), and anti-Mormon framings appear **only as distractors**.

## A. Leadership & succession  (HIGH currency — good training-cutoff discriminators)

- **President Russell M. Nelson died September 27, 2025**, age 101.
- **Dallin H. Oaks is the current (17th) President of the Church**, set apart
  October 2025 (sustained in a solemn assembly, April 2026 general conference).
- **First Presidency (2026):** Pres. Dallin H. Oaks; 1st counselor **Henry B. Eyring**;
  2nd counselor **D. Todd Christofferson**.
- **Jeffrey R. Holland died** (had been President of the Quorum of the Twelve).
- **Dieter F. Uchtdorf** — Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve (named Jan 2026).
- New apostles: **Gérald Caussé** (ordained Nov 2025), **Clark G. Gilbert** (called Feb 2026).
- **Succession principle:** on a President's death the First Presidency dissolves; the
  **most senior apostle** (longest-tenured member of the Quorum of the Twelve) becomes
  President of the Church — *not* the first counselor automatically, *not* by election.
- Source: Church News general-authority chart (Mar/Apr 2026); churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/first-presidency.

## B. Name of the Church  (Aug & Oct 2018, Pres. Nelson)

- Official name: **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints** (given by
  revelation in 1838 — D&C 115:4).
- Short references encouraged: **"the Church"** or **"the Church of Jesus Christ."**
- **Discouraged:** "Mormon Church," "LDS Church," "Church of the Latter-day Saints,"
  and "Mormons"/"LDS" as substitutes. Members: **"Latter-day Saints"** / "members of
  the Church of Jesus Christ." ("Mormon"/"LDS" → distractor or anti-pattern.)
- Source: newsroom style guide; Nelson, "The Correct Name of the Church," Oct 2018.

## C. Word of Wisdom clarification  (New Era, Aug 2019; D&C 89)

- **Prohibited:** vaping / e-cigarettes; **coffee** (incl. lattes, mochas, espresso,
  anything "-ccino"); **all tea from the tea plant — green AND black** (same plant).
- Herbal teas are not prohibited by that rule. **Marijuana & opioids:** only medicinal,
  as prescribed by a competent physician.
- Classic D&C 89 elements: no wine/strong drink, tobacco, "hot drinks" (=coffee & tea);
  grain, herbs, fruit; meat "sparingly"; promises incl. "run and not be weary."
- Source: newsroom "Statement on the Word of Wisdom," Aug 2019.

## D. Recent organizational changes (currency)

- **Ministering** (April 2018) replaced **home teaching & visiting teaching**; flexible,
  not a monthly prepared lesson; **quarterly ministering interviews**; young women 14–18
  serve as companions (paralleling young men).
- **Two-hour Sunday block** + **Come, Follow Me** home-centered/Church-supported study
  began **Jan 2019**.
- **Children and Youth** program began **Jan 1, 2020**, replacing Scouting (BSA
  relationship ended Dec 31, 2019), **Personal Progress, Duty to God, Faith in God,**
  and Activity Days.
- **Temple recommend questions** revised **Oct 2019** (verbiage of ~11 of the 15
  questions clarified; Q12–13 omitted for youth limited-use recommends; worthiness
  standards unchanged).
- **Temple garment** redesigned options (open-sleeve/tank-style) authorized **Oct 2024**
  for hot/humid climates; broader U.S. availability rolling out from Q4 2025.
- Missionary minimum ages lowered **Oct 2012** (men 18, women 19).

## F. Stable doctrinal anchors (author keys to these)

- **Godhead:** three **separate, distinct beings**, one in purpose/will; the Father and
  Son have glorified bodies of flesh and bone, the Holy Ghost is a personage of spirit
  (D&C 130:22). *Not* the Nicene Trinity (→ `protestant_trap`).
- **Degrees of glory:** **Celestial, Terrestrial, Telestial** (1 Cor 15; D&C 76); sons
  of perdition inherit no glory. Celestial has three heavens; exaltation in the highest.
- **Plan of Salvation:** premortal life → birth/veil → mortality/probation → spirit
  world (paradise & prison) → resurrection → final judgment → degrees of glory.
- **Intelligences** co-eternal with God, **organized into spirits** (Abraham 3:22;
  D&C 93:29) — rejects creation *ex nihilo* (→ `protestant_trap`/`folk_doctrine_trap`).
- **Kolob** (Abraham 3:3) is the star/governing body **nearest to where God dwells** —
  *not* the planet God lives on (common `folk_doctrine_trap`).
- **Standard works:** Bible (KJV; AoF 8: "as far as it is translated correctly"),
  Book of Mormon, Doctrine & Covenants, Pearl of Great Price.
- **Book of Mormon:** translated by **Joseph Smith** "by the gift and power of God";
  published 1830; "Another Testament of Jesus Christ"; keystone of our religion; Three
  and Eight Witnesses.
- **Restoration timeline:** First Vision **1820** (Joseph Smith, age 14); Moroni 1823;
  Aaronic Priesthood restored by **John the Baptist (May 15, 1829)**, Melchizedek by
  **Peter, James, and John**; Church organized **April 6, 1830** (Fayette, NY).
- **Martyrdom:** Joseph & Hyrum Smith killed at **Carthage Jail, June 27, 1844**;
  succession to **Brigham Young / Quorum of the Twelve** (1844 "transfiguration"
  accounts). Pioneers entered the **Salt Lake Valley July 24, 1847**.
- **Plural marriage** ended officially by the **1890 Manifesto (OD-1, Wilford Woodruff)**;
  Second Manifesto 1904. The **priesthood/temple restriction on Black members was lifted
  June 1978 (OD-2, Spencer W. Kimball)**; the Race & the Priesthood essay disavows past
  theories used to justify it.
- **Temple ordinances:** baptism & confirmation, the endowment, sealing of families,
  and **baptism/ordinances for the dead** (vicarious work; 1 Cor 15:29; D&C 128).
- **Covenant living:** **tithing** = one-tenth of increase; fast offerings; law of
  chastity; Sabbath observance; the **Articles of Faith** (13; Joseph Smith, 1842
  Wentworth letter).
- **Foundational proclamations:** *The Family: A Proclamation to the World* (1995);
  *The Living Christ* (2000); *The Restoration Proclamation* (2020).

## G. Cultural-fluency anchors (lived practice, for CF items)

- A **bishop** is called by the **stake president** through inspiration and **sustained**
  (not elected) by the ward; serves ~5 years, unpaid lay clergy.
- "**RM**" = returned missionary; missions ~24 months (men) / ~18 months (women); MTC;
  companionships; "greenie"; "trunky"; transfers; P-day.
- **Temple recommend** = certification of worthiness via two interviews (bishop + stake
  presidency) to enter the temple; biennial renewal.
- **BYU** Honor Code (no alcohol/coffee/tea, dress/grooming standards, curfew in housing,
  chastity); "ring by spring"; high marriage rate.
- "**I prayed about it**" frames decisions as seeking personal revelation/confirmation
  (the "burning in the bosom," D&C 9) rather than mere preference.
- **Ward/stake** geography-based; **callings** are assignments accepted by faith;
  **Relief Society, Elders Quorum, Primary, Young Women/Men, Sunday School**.
- "**Spirit vs. letter of the law**": Word-of-Wisdom and Sabbath decisions often turn on
  intent and discipleship, not legalistic checklists.
